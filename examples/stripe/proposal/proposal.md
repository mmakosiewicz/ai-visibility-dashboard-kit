# What to track for Stripe in AI answers

We read the 50 questions this report tracks, sorted them into 8 topics, and checked which numbers are worth putting on a dashboard for each one, using 600 real AI answers from 2 days.

**Good fit for a dashboard.** Most questions can be measured. Each topic below has at least one number worth tracking.

> Numbers here come from a small sample. They show whether something is worth tracking, not what the real figure is. “3 of 12” doesn't mean 25%.

## What kinds of questions are in this report

| Questions | Type | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 42 | Asks how to do something | Wants steps or help with a job (e.g. “how do I …?”) |
| 4 | Asks for recommendations | Wants names: the best, which one, where to go (e.g. “best … for …”, “which … should I …?”) |
| 2 | About a competitor | Names a competitor, not Stripe (e.g. “is [competitor] any good?”) |
| 2 | General question | Wants information, not a recommendation (e.g. “what is …?”, “is … worth it?”) |

The type decides what can be measured: Stripe can only “win” a question that asks for a recommendation.

## Worth checking

- 3 of 50 questions were hard to sort. They're marked ⚠ in each topic's question list and are worth a quick look.

## Choose a payment provider for your business

### Worth tracking

**73%** **How often Stripe is named** · *main number*  
Out of all answers, how many name Stripe in the text (a link to its site alone doesn't count).  
`Mention rate = answers that name Stripe ÷ all answers`  
`= 44 ÷ 60 = 73%`  
About 210 answers a week to count, enough to see real changes.

**71%** **Stripe's share of recommendations**  
When an answer recommends someone specific, how often that includes Stripe.  
`Capture = answers that recommend Stripe ÷ answers that recommend any specific brand or organization`  
`= 34 ÷ 48 = 71%`  
About 168 answers a week to count, enough to see real changes.

**3%** **How often Stripe's website is a source**  
Of all the websites AI cites as sources (each counted once per answer), the share that are Stripe's own.  
`Owned citation share = cited websites that are Stripe's ÷ all cited websites (each counted once per answer)`  
`= 7 ÷ 235 = 3%`  
About 822 cited websites a week to count, enough to see real changes.

**Whose websites AI uses as sources**  
Sources split into Stripe's site, competitors' sites, forums and everyone else.  
`Citation split = cited websites in each group ÷ all cited websites (each counted once per answer)`  
`= everyone else 81%, forums & social 10%, competitors' sites 5%, Stripe's site 2% of 235 cited websites`  
About 822 cited websites a week to count, enough to see real changes.

## How to read this
